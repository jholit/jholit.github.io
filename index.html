Project / 01

Gamers Ultra Plus

Independent redesign

A responsive storefront prototype that gives a busy retail homepage one clear job: help shoppers discover, compare, and explore games.

My contribution
Information architecture, content hierarchy, interface editing, responsive design, and prototype direction.
Format
Interactive front-end prototype; not client work.

Problem

Product discovery, promotions, support, and company content competed for attention instead of guiding shoppers through one clear retail task.

Decision

I layered the product information: a featured game introduces the offer, search and filters support discovery, cards make comparison easy, and deeper details open on demand. Support, policy, and newsletter content remain in the footer.

Observable change

The homepage now moves directly from discovery to comparison to product details, with calls to action that match what the prototype opens.

Project / 02

HOLIX Ai

Independent concept

An interactive AI workspace concept exploring how added files, projects, tools, and controls can remain understandable without overwhelming the core chat experience.

My contribution
Interface direction, information architecture, interaction model, responsive behavior, content decisions, and prototype direction.
Format
Interactive interface prototype; not client work or a connected AI product.

Problem

Adding application tools to a chat interface can create a wall of equally prominent controls, making navigation and conversation harder to follow.

Decision

I separated persistent navigation from contextual tasks. The fixed rail gives core destinations stable locations, while temporary tools open beside the action that triggered them and then recede. The composer remains focused on writing, voice, and send.

Observable change

Core destinations remain predictable while task-specific controls no longer compete with the conversation. Messages, replies, and metadata also carry distinct visual weight.

About Jaqweal Holit
